How it works

  1. 1

    Add your music

    A title and an artist are enough. Everything you don't know stays UNKNOWN.

  2. 2

    Discover

    REACH runs dozens of live web searches built from your track's profile, through a hardened crawler that respects robots.txt and drops bot walls.

  3. 3

    Review the evidence

    Qualified outlets arrive scored, deduplicated, and sourced. Scam sites arrive BLOCKED, with reasons.

  4. 4

    Approve outreach

    Every pitch is built only from sourced facts. You read the exact message; editing it cancels any approval.

  5. 5

    Get responses

    Replies, placements and follow-ups tracked per outlet — placements only count with an evidence URL.

What REACH refuses to do

Most outreach tools compete on what they automate. REACH competes on what it won't.

✕

No pay-for-play

Guaranteed-streams and guaranteed-placement sites are BLOCKED outright, with the evidence attached.

✕

No sending without you

Every email needs your explicit approval, bound to a fingerprint of the exact message. Sending stays locked until your domain passes real DNS checks.

✕

No invented numbers

Stream counts come from your own reported figures. Fit claims come from the outlet's own pages. Nothing is padded to look better.

✕

No CAPTCHA solving, no login walls

Outlets that need forms, logins or platform tools route to you as prepared tasks — with your answers copy-ready.

✕

No automated DMs, no scraping private pages

Only public pages, crawled politely: robots.txt honored, rate limits kept, private interfaces untouched.

✕

No silent failures

A provider without credentials says NOT CONNECTED. A refusal states its reason. Demo data is labeled on every screen.

Where REACH honestly is

Early software, in active development. Each REACH instance serves one artist or team. Discovery, evidence, scoring and drafting work today; email sending unlocks only after your sending domain passes SPF, DKIM and DMARC checks — and that gate is the product working, not a limitation of it.
