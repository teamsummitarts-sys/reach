The workflow

Find. Qualify. Approve. Track.

The point is not to generate another contact list. REACH turns a release into an evidence-backed campaign: what is open, why it fits, what should happen next, and what actually happened.

01 / DISCOVER

Find live opportunities

Search the open web for playlists, press, radio, blogs, creators, curators, and other routes relevant to the track.

02 / QUALIFY

Know why it fits

Match the release against genre, context, territory, freshness, credibility, submission status, and source evidence.

03 / EXECUTE

Move with control

Prepare pitches and submission tasks, keep human approval in the loop, and route login-required or manual forms to the right person.

04 / LEARN

Track the outcome

Record submissions, replies, placements, follow-ups, relationship history, and evidence so each release improves the next one.
